6 · SiteID drilldown and actions

Spend is not available per siteid. Site ROAS is estimated as D7 CM1 ÷ (campaign spend × the site's share of campaign logins), which assumes equal cost per login inside a campaign. Treat it as a ranking signal. Sites under 100 logins in the last 4 weeks are only flagged when value is zero. Rules: Scale up = est. ROAS ≥ 1.25× campaign and Dep CVR ≥ 16%, or Dep CVR ≥ 18% with L20 ≥ 14% and ROAS at or above campaign. Scale down = est. ROAS ≤ 0.65× campaign, or ROAS under 3% with Dep CVR under 14%.
