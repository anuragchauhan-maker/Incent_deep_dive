    VI. Notes

    Read before acting

    • InboxDollars moved from the Prodege siteid to vc-* codes on 23 Mar 2026. That is a provider reporting change, not an acquisition event. Everything here is after the change.
    • FreeCash spend sits under Almedia campaign names (no "freecash" keyword). It was matched on "almedia". Spend and logins both start in the week of 31 Aug, so its prior-4 window is one partial week.
    • KashKick had no US logins or spend in the window.
    • ROAS is channel level only. The spend table has no siteid, and siteid CM1 per login is a quality proxy rather than a return on spend. D7 CM1 is lumpy, a single large payer can move a small siteid for a week.
    • Siteids under 200 logins in the last 4 weeks are marked TEST or HOLD, not scale decisions.
