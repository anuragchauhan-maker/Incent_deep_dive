Read first. D7 ROAS is far below the 10% red line for every channel, so channels are ranked against each other. Spend is not recorded by siteid: siteid ROAS is estimated by pricing each login at its campaign's average cost per login for the same window. FreeCash has no matched spend (campaign names carry no spend rows), so it is judged on quality and CM1 per login only. KashKick had no US logins in the window.
