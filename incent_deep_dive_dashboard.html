Incent Deep Dive

US incent channels · D7-matured cohorts only · generated 9 Oct 2026 · latest matured week = login week of 21 Sep; last 4 matured = 31 Aug–27 Sep (L4), prior 4 = 3–30 Aug (P4)
Weeks from 28 Sep are excluded because their D7 window had not closed at run time. Dep CVR = D0–D7 depositors ÷ all logins. Lvl>20 = Player logins reaching level 21+ by D7. ROAS = D7 CM1 ÷ spend. Platform benchmark = all US channels incl. organic. KashKick had no US activity in the window.

Summary

  • Incent ROAS is low everywhere. L4 incent blended D7 ROAS is 6.6% (P4 4.1%) against a platform 18.3%. No channel reaches the 10% line except AdAction Bingo in the latest week, so scale decisions below are relative within incent.
  • MyAppFree: scale-down worked. Spend −40% (121k → 72.5k), Dep CVR 15.4% → 21.5% (above platform), Lvl>20 11.9% → 15.7%, ROAS 2.0% → 4.3%. Latest week spend rebounded to 19.9k with ROAS 2.3%, so do not add budget at channel level.
  • AdAction: best ROAS in incent. Spend −20%, ROAS 5.0% → 9.1%, but Dep CVR fell 15.7% → 12.3% and the latest-week volume is half of earlier weeks.
  • InboxDollars: wasted scale. Spend +15% (61k → 70.6k), ROAS flat at 7.4%, Dep CVR 14.1% → 13.0%, Lvl>20 11.2% → 9.4%. Latest week Bingo Lvl>20 is 5.0% on flat spend. Cause is concentrated in siteid vc-20229 Bingo.
  • FreeCash is the weakest on quality. Launched 31 Aug (Almedia Bingo iOS), 38k spend, Dep CVR 11.9%, Lvl>20 7.3%, ROAS 5.9%. High volume at the lowest CPL, but the lowest progression.
