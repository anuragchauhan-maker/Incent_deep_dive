Method and data notes

  • Dep CVR: deposits in D0 to D7 over all logins. Lvl>20: player logins reaching level 21+ by D7. D7 ROAS: D7 CM1 over spend. US only.
  • SiteID SCALE UP needs est. ROAS at least 1.3x its channel, or at least 1.0x with Dep CVR and Lvl>20 both at least 1.3x. SCALE DOWN needs est. ROAS at most 0.7x. Under 100 logins in the last 4 weeks is marked directional. Only siteIDs with 60+ logins across the 8 weeks are listed.
  • FreeCash: spend rows do not match the campaign keyword (campaign is named "Almedia"), so spend and ROAS are blank. The channel started at scale in the week of Aug 31, so the prior 4 weeks hold one partial week.
  • InboxDollars moved from Prodege to vc-* siteIDs on 2026-03-23. This is a reporting change. It does not affect the periods shown here.
  • KashKick had no logins or spend in the window. MyAppFree siteIDs 1318, 1573 (Solitaire) and 3950 stopped before Sep 7.
