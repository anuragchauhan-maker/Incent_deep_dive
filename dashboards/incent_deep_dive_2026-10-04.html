IV · Site ID drilldown

Spend is booked per campaign, not per site ID. Site-level ROAS allocates each campaign's spend across its site IDs by share of logins, so it measures CM1 per login relative to the campaign's cost per login. Flags compare each site with its own campaign on Dep CVR, Lvl>20 and allocated ROAS (L4 window). Sites under 100 L4 logins are marked low-confidence; sites with 0 L4 logins are inactive.

Notes

  • Dep CVR uses all logins (no user-type filter). Lvl>20 uses Player logins only. Both use D0–D7 windows. Platform benchmark is every channel in the US, organic included.
  • InboxDollars moved from the single Prodege site ID to vc-* codes on 23 Mar 2026. That is a reporting change, so it does not affect the windows shown here (all after the change).
  • Site IDs come from the latest install record per appsflyer_id. Some logins have no site ID; those are not shown in the site table.
  • AdAction Bingo ROAS is inflated by a few high-CM1 users in two site IDs (329831306, 329832031). They are held rather than scaled.
  • All incent ROAS sits well under the platform figure. The decisions here are relative: move volume from weaker site IDs to stronger ones within the same campaign.
