6 · Recommendations — scale up / scale down

Rules (L4, ≥100 L4 logins): SCALE UP = ROAS ≥8% & CVR ≥15%, or CVR ≥ platform+3pp & Lvl>20 ≥ platform & ROAS ≥ channel (need ≥150 logins). SCALE DOWN = ROAS <4.5% & CVR < platform; or already-cut siteid with P4 ROAS <4.5%. Incent ROAS is structurally far below platform (~6% vs ~24%), so thresholds are relative to incent blend, not the 10/30% platform colouring.
