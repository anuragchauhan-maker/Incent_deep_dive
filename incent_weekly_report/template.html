Data notes

  • Spend is joined by campaign-name keyword. FreeCash spend sits under the Almedia campaign name and starts the week of Aug 31, so its P5-8 window has 3 weeks. KashKick had no US activity in this window.

  • Dep CVR uses all logins. Level>20 uses Player logins only. D0–D7 inclusive. Platform benchmark is every US channel, organic included.
  • Siteids with fewer than 200 logins over the 8 weeks are left out.

Siteid scorecard and action

Verdicts use the last 4 matured weeks. Scale up: implied ROAS ≥ 10% with CVR ≥ 15% and Level>20 ≥ 12%, or CVR ≥ 20% with Level>20 ≥ 13% and ROAS at or above the channel. Scale down: CVR < 12% with Level>20 < 8%, or Level>20 ≤ 6.5% with ROAS < 5.5%, or ROAS < 2.5%. Under 100 logins is a watch, not an action.
