5 · SiteID scorecard & recommendation

Rules: Scale up = last-4 ROAS ≥1.25× incent avg, CVR or Lvl>20 ≥ avg, ROAS ≥avg in ≥2 of 4 weeks and latest week not collapsing. Scale down = ROAS ≤0.6× avg, or below avg with both CVR and Lvl>20 >15% under avg, or latest-week ROAS <0.6× avg with weak quality. Needs ≥150 logins (siteid) / ≥100 (siteid×campaign) in last-4; <60 logins in latest 2 weeks = dormant.

Notes & method

  • Dep CVR = D0–D7 depositors / all logins (no Player filter). Lvl>20 = player logins reaching level 21+ within D7 (capped at next reinstall). ROAS = D7 CM1 / spend. Green ≥30%, red <10% convention applies — all incent ROAS here is below 10% except as noted.
  • FreeCash = Almedia Bingo campaign (spend matched on "almedia"; launched week of 31 Aug, so no prior-4 comparison). FreeCash logins with NULL campaign excluded from siteid views.
  • Platform ROAS divides platform D7 CM1 by all marketing spend in the spend table (not incent only).
