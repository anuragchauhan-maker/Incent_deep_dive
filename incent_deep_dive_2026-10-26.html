    Method and caveats

    Dep CVR: deposits D0–D7 / all logins (no user_type filter). Lvl>20: Player logins reaching level 21+ by D7, window capped at next login. ROAS: D7 CM1 / spend. US only.
    FreeCash logins start 31 Aug in this window, so its prior-4 volume is understated.
    InboxDollars moved from Prodege to vc-* siteids on 23 Mar 2026. That is a reporting change, not an acquisition event.
    Siteid cells under 15 logins in a period were dropped by the query; latest-week and previous-week siteid values exist only where a cell passed that threshold, so week-on-week siteid reads are partial.
    Small cells (100–150 logins) are lumpy because single high payers move CM1; these are tagged “test”.
