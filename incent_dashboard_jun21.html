⬇️ SCALE DOWN — InboxDollars

Rising spend (+19.8%) with falling quality (Dep CVR: 14.3%→12.6%, now below platform benchmark of 16.1%). Quality drag is concentrated in two siteids:

  • vc-20229: 3,994 logins W1–W4, Dep CVR 10.9%, Lvl 8.7% → primary culprit driving ~40% of volume at worst-in-class quality. Recommend: PAUSE or hard cap.
  • vc-36755: 1,898 logins, Dep CVR 7.2%, Lvl 5.7% → second worst. Recommend: PAUSE.
  • vc-18478 & vc-32188: Consistently below benchmark. Reduce allocation.

Campaigns affected: InboxDollars Bingo, InboxDollars Solitaire, InboxDollars GinRummy (all active campaigns under inboxdollars_int).

⬆️ SCALE UP — MyAppFree (site 3739)

MyAppFree quality consistently 6–8pp above platform benchmark (Dep CVR ~22–25%, Lvl >20%). Site 3739 is the dominant driver:

  • 3739: 4,534 logins W1–W4, Dep CVR 25.3%, Lvl 20.3% → BEST quality site with GROWING volume (W1: 1,628 logins, +17% WoW). Scale up allocation.
  • GinRummy organic: High dep_cvr (22–39%) with zero spend — explore paid GinRummy campaigns at MyAppFree.

Campaigns affected: MyAppFree Bingo, MyAppFree Solitaire. Add MyAppFree GinRummy campaigns.

⬆️ SCALE UP — AdAction (Solitaire/Bingo)

Quality improving quarter over quarter: Dep CVR up from 13.7% (W5–W8) to 16.6% (W1–W4), now at platform benchmark. Spend has declined 9% — room to scale back up.

  • 329830666: Largest siteid, quality improved from 9.6% to 16.3% Dep CVR. Scale Solitaire budget.
  • 329831306: Was 27.9% Dep CVR but volume has dried up — explore reactivation.
  • 329825370: Volatile — had severe negative CM1 spike in W3 (May 18). Monitor closely before scaling.

Campaigns affected: AdAction Solitaire (primary), AdAction Bingo.

⚠️ INVESTIGATE — FreeCash Pause

FreeCash was the highest ROAS channel in the portfolio (up to 32% in Apr 20 week) with Dep CVR of 19–29%, well above platform benchmark. It was effectively paused after May 11.

  • W5–W8 avg ROAS: 17.2% (vs InboxDollars 4.0%, AdAction 2.7%)
  • Dep CVR: 19.6–28.7% across all measured weeks
  • GinRummy category drove highest quality: 25–30% dep_cvr, ROAS up to 38%

Investigate reason for pause. If supply-side, engage FreeCash account team. If internal budget decision, recommend reversal — this is your most efficient incent dollar.

🔒 MAINTAIN / DO NOT SCALE — InboxDollars vc-20229 & vc-36755 (Priority)

These two siteids alone account for ~5,892 logins in W1–W4 (54% of InboxDollars volume) at quality of 7–11% Dep CVR, vs platform benchmark of 16.1%. They are the primary reason InboxDollars has crossed below benchmark. While InboxDollars has two high-quality siteids to protect (vc-18936: 25.6% CVR, vc-22484: 22.4% CVR), the low-quality volume overwhelms the average. Immediate action required.
